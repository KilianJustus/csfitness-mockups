About us

Boutique fitness.
Personal by design.

CS Fitness is a boutique EMS studio in Henderson, Nevada — built on one belief: everybody deserves a workout that fits their life.

Our story

Why we built CS Fitness

Most people don't fail at fitness because they lack willpower — they fail because traditional gyms demand hours they don't have. CS Fitness flips that equation: cutting-edge EMS technology, one certified coach per member, twenty minutes per week.

Every detail of the studio is designed around you: private sessions instead of crowded floors, personal coaching instead of anonymous machines, and measurable progress instead of guesswork.
