Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Is EMS training safe?
Yes — EMS is low-impact, joint-friendly and always supervised by certified coaches. The technology is well-researched and FDA-cleared.
How does EMS work?
During a session you wear a vest, belt and straps with electrodes on all major muscle groups. Controlled impulses trigger muscle contractions — activating more muscle fibers than traditional workouts, in just 20 minutes.
Will EMS help me lose weight?
Absolutely. Each session burns up to 500 calories and triggers a 48–72 hour afterburn effect, so your body keeps burning fat long after you leave the studio.
