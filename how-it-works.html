The EMS Method

How EMS works

Electro Muscle Stimulation mimics the natural signals your brain sends during exercise — just more of them, everywhere at once.

What is EMS?

Your whole body.
One session.

During a session you wear a vest, belt and straps equipped with electrodes placed on all major muscle groups. Controlled electrical impulses cause your muscles to contract and relax — mimicking the natural signals your brain sends during exercise, but activating a much higher percentage of muscle fibers.

The result: an efficient full-body workout that builds strength, endurance and muscle tone in just 20 minutes — guided 1-on-1 by a certified coach who adjusts the intensity for every muscle group individually.

⚡ Up to 90% muscle activation ✓ FDA-cleared 🕐 20 minutes total
