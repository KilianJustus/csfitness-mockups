The EMS Method

How EMS works

Electro Muscle Stimulation mimics the natural signals your brain sends during exercise — just more of them, everywhere at once.

What is EMS?

Your whole body.
One session.

During a session you wear a vest, belt and straps equipped with electrodes placed on all major muscle groups. Controlled electrical impulses cause your muscles to contract and relax — mimicking the natural signals your brain sends during exercise, but activating a much higher percentage of muscle fibers.

The result: an efficient full-body workout that builds strength, endurance and muscle tone in just 20 minutes — guided by your personal coach, who adjusts the intensity for every muscle group individually.

Safety first

Well-researched.
Professionally supervised.

EMS has been used in physiotherapy and professional sports for decades. Our technology is FDA-cleared, and every single session is supervised by a certified coach — nobody ever trains alone. CS FITNESS is using the same pulses to create a full-body workout experience, making the incredible benefits available for the everyday individual.

Please note: EMS is not suitable for everyone (e.g. pregnancy, pacemakers, severe neurological conditions). Your free intro session starts with a short health screening — we'll check together whether EMS is right for you.
