Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What is EMS training?
EMS (Electro Muscle Stimulation) uses controlled electrical impulses to stimulate muscle contractions. You wear a vest, belt and straps with electrodes on all major muscle groups, mimicking the natural signals your brain sends during exercise but activating a much higher percentage of muscle fibers.
Is EMS training safe?
Yes. EMS is low-impact, minimizing joint stress, and is always supervised by one of our certified coaches. The technology is well-researched and FDA-cleared.
What results can I expect?
You can expect noticeable improvements in muscle tone, strength and endurance within a few weeks. Many members also experience better posture, increased metabolism and overall body shaping.
Will EMS help me lose weight?
Each session burns up to 500 calories and triggers a lasting afterburn effect, so your body keeps burning fat long after you leave the studio.
What do I need to bring?
Just yourself. We provide your personal TENCEL training outfit, pH-balanced water and fresh towels. Everything is included in every session.
Who is EMS not suitable for?
EMS is not suitable during pregnancy, with a pacemaker or severe neurological conditions. Your free intro session always starts with a short health screening, so we can make sure together that EMS is right for you.
